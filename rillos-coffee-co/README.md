# Rillo's Coffee Co.

Single-page site for Rillo's nitro cold brew: a hero pour video, our story, meet the founder, where to find us (Flock & Flourish, Bellingham, MA), and a call to action for serving Rillo's at stores, offices and events.

It's a static site with no build step. Open `index.html` in a browser, or serve the folder with any static host (GitHub Pages works as-is).

- `index.html` holds the page, styles and script
- `media/` holds the pour video and poster, the coffee beans, the founder photo, the Flock & Flourish logo and the icons

Placeholders still to swap: the pour video (synthesized, to be replaced with filmed footage), the founder copy, and the Instagram and Facebook links in the footer.
